# Roadmap

## Coming soon: Live Sales / Auctions

A virtual yard sale experience where sellers can go live, showcase items in real time, and
neighbors can bid or claim items on the spot. This is **not implemented yet**; the home page
only shows a teaser.

Ideas to explore:
- Live video or photo-stream "sale rooms" tied to a `yardSaleEvents` document
- Real-time bidding with a countdown timer (Firestore listeners)
- Seller tools: start/stop live session, mark items sold
- Notifications when a followed seller goes live

## Other ideas
- Expand `CITIES` in `src/lib/constants.ts` (add a matching `CITY_CENTERS` entry)
- Favorites and saved searches
